# Minutes Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

## Local data

Minutes records audio, creates transcripts, and runs supported local AI models on your device. Recordings, transcripts, summaries, preferences, and model files are stored locally in locations controlled by you.

This distribution does not configure or transmit usage analytics. The legacy analytics integration remains disabled and has no project key or collection destination configured.

## Optional external services

Minutes can connect to external AI providers only when you choose one and provide its configuration or credentials. Content sent through those features is processed under the selected provider's privacy policy and terms. Local providers do not require that transfer.

The updater contacts GitHub Releases at `praveenvnktsh/minutes` to check for signed application updates. GitHub may receive ordinary network metadata associated with that request.

Local models are downloaded from Hugging Face when you choose or accept them, including the live notes and wrap-up adapters (`praveenvnktsh00/minutes-notes-gemma4-e2b`, `praveenvnktsh00/minutes-tidy-gemma4-e2b`), which are fetched once the Gemma 4 E2B model is on disk. Hugging Face may receive ordinary network metadata associated with those downloads. No meeting content is sent.

## Your control

You control where recordings are saved, which models and providers are used, and when local meeting data is deleted. Back up or remove local data using normal filesystem and application controls.

## Security

Minutes relies on operating-system permissions and filesystem protections. No software can guarantee absolute security; review the source and use device encryption when meeting data is sensitive.
