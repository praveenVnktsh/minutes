    How it works

    Your notes, finished for you.

    Most AI notetakers throw away what you wrote and hand you a generic report. Minutes starts from your notes — your words, your order — and fills in what you didn't have time to type.

    Privacy

    Airplane mode is a feature.

    Your meetings hold salaries, roadmaps, diagnoses and deals. They belong on your disk, not in somebody's training set.

    Transcribed on your machine.Whisper and Parakeet run locally, accelerated by your GPU.

    No account, no telemetry.Usage telemetry is off and there's no analytics destination configured. Nothing to sign up for.

    Cloud AI is opt-in, and obvious.Only if you add your own provider key does anything leave, and only to the provider you chose.

    Open source, so you don't have to trust us.Every line is on GitHub under the MIT license. Read it, build it, audit it.
